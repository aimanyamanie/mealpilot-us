# 2026-10-29: Re-scope to the PRD v2: a US catalogue database only

Supersedes the product parts of `2026-10-29-mvp-and-schema.md`: the web app,
30-day plan, and the planner and target port. Its schema reasoning still stands.

## What changed
Medina's PRD v2 (2026-10-26) and Arini's design handoff arrived after the first plan:
- **The product:** one mobile app (iOS + Android) for both Indonesia and the US, on
  one codebase and account system. Plans are **weekly** (7 days × breakfast, lunch,
  dinner, plus an optional snack), not 30-day.
- **The design:** Arini's iOS prototype files are already the US market version
  (English, USD, ft/lb, USDA).
- **Aiman's role is the database.** The app, its user data, the plan generator and
  Veny belong to the app team.

## Decided with Aiman
1. **This repo is the catalogue only:** chefs, recipes, ingredients, nutrition, tags,
   cost and meal-prep info. Users, plans, tracking, subscriptions and Veny live in
   the app team's database.
2. **It's a separate US database** (Neon, US East), not an extension of
   `mealpilot-data`. `market` is on `creators` and `recipes` so the two catalogues
   can be merged later.
3. **The planner and target port session was stopped.** Its `web/` folder is left
   uncommitted; Aiman decides whether to delete it.

## Fields added to match the PRD and design
| Need | Source | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Sugar | US-09, TC-19 | `ingredients.sugar_100g`, `recipe_nutrition.per_serving_sugar` |
| Prep, cook minutes and difficulty | US-09, design | `recipes.prep_minutes`, `cook_minutes`, `difficulty` |
| Fridge and freezer days, reheat | US-09, US-13, NE-20 | `recipes.fridge_days`, `freezer_days`, `storage_source`, `reheat_method` |
| Household portion | US-09 | `recipes.portion_description` |
| Cost per serving (USD) | US-09, US-14 | `ingredients.price_usd` per `shop_unit`, then `recipe_nutrition.per_serving_cost_usd`, `cost_coverage` |
| Steps (written by us) | F5, US-09 | `steps` table, `v_recipe_steps`. `source_text` is never exposed |
| Hands-off steps, for prep day | US-13 | `steps.is_hands_off` |
| Video removed, private or region-blocked | NE-15 | `recipes.video_status`, `video_checked_at` |
| Guest samples | US-04 | `recipes.is_guest_sample` |
| Substitutes | NE-37, design | `ingredient_substitutes` |
| Reference data version | NE-39 | `ingredients.source_version`, `recipe_nutrition.reference_version` |
| Nutritionist review | PRD, nutrition accuracy | `recipes.nutrition_reviewed_by` / `_at`, required by `v_published_recipes` |
| FDA Daily Values | PRD, market config | `daily_values` table |
| No pork, no beef, no seafood, halal, kosher | US-05, market config | `animal_source` splits out pork and beef; `has_alcohol`; tags in `v_recipe_flags` |
| Chef card | design | `creators.tagline`, `channel_name`, `subscriber_count` |

**Publish gate (`v_published_recipes`):** a recipe needs all of the following, or it
isn't visible and can't be planned (NE-16):
- the creator's permission;
- a nutritionist sign-off;
- all 7 nutrition fields;
- at least one step, and every step rewritten.

An unavailable video doesn't hide the recipe (NE-15); `video_status` is exposed for
the app to show "Video unavailable".

## Deliberately left to others
- **Budget tier ($ / $$ / $$$):** the view exposes `per_serving_cost_usd`. The tier
  cut-offs are a product decision.
- **Shopping list merging, plan generation, swaps and prep-day sessions:** app-side
  logic. The catalogue gives them buyable units (`shop_unit`, `shop_unit_grams`), store
  sections, grams per serving, `ingredient_ids` for overlap, and hands-off steps.
